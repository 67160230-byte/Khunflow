# KhunFlow brand assets

Original vector identity: K monogram + leaf / growth path.

- Primary green: #087F46
- Mint accent: #A7F3C0
- Dark text: #172D25
- White: #FFFFFF

Use khunflow-horizontal.svg on light backgrounds and khunflow-on-dark.svg on dark backgrounds. Use the mark alone for browser tabs, avatars and small spaces. Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the mark width. Do not stretch or recolor individual parts. Menu/product photos are separate from the brand logo. SVG wordmarks use Arial/Helvetica/system sans fonts.
